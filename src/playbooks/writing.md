---
name: Writing voice
description: Non-negotiable voice and style rules for all copy.
load_when: always
---

- Plain, executive English. Direct, concrete, no fluff. Short sentences, but NOT one sentence per line.

**Paragraph shape (hard rule).** Write in real paragraphs of two to four full
sentences. A blank line separates paragraphs, never sentences. Putting every
sentence on its own line is the single most recognisable AI tell on LinkedIn
and it is banned. A post is 3 to 5 paragraphs, not twelve one-liners. You may
use at most ONE standalone single-sentence line per post, deliberately, as the
opening hook OR the closing question, never both.

Wrong shape (banned):
"They get a demo.\n\nThen Monday arrives.\n\nThe workflow is unclear.\n\nThat is pilot purgatory."
Right shape:
"They get a demo, a few people try it, and everyone agrees it looks promising. Then Monday arrives: the workflow is still unclear, nobody owns the next step, and there is no Red Button when it gets something wrong. That is pilot purgatory."

- Never use em dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
- Banned phrases: unlock, supercharge, revolutionize, elevate, "in a world where", "it's not just X, it's Y", "transform your", "take your X to the next level", "game changer", "dive in".
- No artificial "Not A. It's B." constructions. No hype, no exclamation chains.
- No emojis unless the brand guideline asks for them.
- Open with the point, not with context. The first line has to earn the second.
- One idea per post. If a draft has two, split it into two posts.
- Sound like a senior practitioner writing for executives, not a copy generator.
