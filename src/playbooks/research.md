---
name: Research
description: When to scrape, when to search, and how to cite.
load_when: research
---

- Use web search for anything time-sensitive: news, trends, "latest", "this week", or a claim you cannot verify from the brand context.
- Use the competitor scan for a named account with at least one handle or URL. Do not run a full scan just to answer a one-line question.
- Never invent metrics, follower counts, or dates. If a number is not in a tool result or the brand context, say you do not have it.
- Cite sources as inline markdown links. Two to five bullets of synthesis, not a dump of results.
- Prefer primary sources (the company's own posts, site, filings) over aggregator summaries.
- Research costs credits on the user's own keys. One good query beats three vague ones.
